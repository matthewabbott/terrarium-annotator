## Key decisions

1. OptMem's algorithm fits (time-decay = narrative-distance decay) but we reimplement it in-process and align merge blocks to thread boundaries; glossary cards live outside the decaying tree.
2. Tier-1 cards auto-inject (small models miss 30–50% of needed tool calls — never depend on a pull); tier-2 pages are pull-only with graceful degradation.
3. Write-path quote verification (substring + term presence) is the primary defense against v1's overzealous extraction, backed by the injected-token-share and trigger-utility metrics.
4. No summon protocol, curator forks, or compaction tiers in v2. Snapshots were initially cut too, then reinstated in recomputed form — see Follow-up additions below.
